## Denver discovery finding

[USFS Winiger Ridge](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/arp/recreation/winiger-ridge) explicitly authorizes overnight use only at designated first-come sites and recommends high clearance. Its national EDW record28368 is classified `Intermediate Parent Group`, so searching only camping activities loses a useful roadside option. The current page has a different coordinate from EDW; both are area markers. [Denver Water](https://www.denverwater.org/recreation/gross-resevoir) supplies practical details: no fees/reservations,14 consecutive days/28 in60, numbered sites only, construction interruptions possible. [Current USFS road table](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/arp/conditions) has a dated OPEN entry; MVUM359.1 and spurs publish May15–November30. The site must preserve that dated evidence rather than convert it into certainty about tonight.

The nearby negative is equally important: Denver Water general recreation/parking is sunrise–sunset. [Jeffco permits](https://www.jeffco.us/1512/Permits-Reservations) explicitly exclude vehicle camping. West Magnolia's older EDW point is30.93 miles and Gordon Gulch32.93 miles straight-line from the chosen center, so neither satisfies this case's bound.

## Glenwood discovery finding

The EDW rectangle around Glenwood contained142 recreation records but no preferred dispersed-car inventory entry within30 miles after inspection. That does not prove there are no legal roadside positions. It proves the current inventory plus this bounded audit does not establish one. The [BLM Thompson Creek page](https://www.blm.gov/visit/thompson-creek-campground) establishes four primitive sites, no fee,7-day April–August /14-day September–March limit, pack-out human waste, and poor wet/snow access. This is classified as a backup campground.

[Fourmile winter gate alert](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/whiteriver/alerts/fourmile-road-winter-gate-closure-black-hills-williams-peak-parking) began five days before this test date. [White River2026-13](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/whiteriver/alerts/white-river-national-forest-camp-location-restrictions) and [Eagle-Holy Cross2025-09](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/whiteriver/alerts/eagle-holy-cross-ranger-district-occupancy-and-use) demonstrate why merely recommending a forest road is insufficient: water/trail100-foot exclusions and specific quarter-mile buffers/corridors must be evaluated.

## Crested Butte discovery finding

[USFS designated camping page](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/gmug/recreation/designated-camping-crested-butte) distinguishes vehicle-accessible dispersed sites outside campground facilities and lists MC1–17 among options accommodating larger vehicles. [GMUG-2022-10](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/gmug/alerts/crested-butte-area-camping-and-fire-prohibitions) is valid through June30,2027. This supports preferred roadside options, not just campground fallback.

The [agency Slate River map](https://www.fs.usda.gov/media/65319), dated April4,2024, has embedded GeoPDF georeferencing. Ten labeled group symbols were manually identified and transformed using that metadata. Raw PDF, rendered image and derived group positions are cached under `research/audit-rockies/`. These carry a conservative200m accuracy field and explicit group scope. They are suitable for locating a group of numbered sites, not pinpointing which patch of ground is legal. Current winter access was not verified, so the correct result is documented permission plus access unknown.

[Horse Ranch Park](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r02/gmug/recreation/horse-ranch-park-dispersed-paonia-rd) is independently documented as a free dispersed area,7-day limit,11 road miles west from Crested Butte; EDW provides its point. Its June season field and absent current Kebler winter status prevent a tonight promise.
